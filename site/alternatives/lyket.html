An alternative to Lyket

Lyket is a hosted service for like, clap and rating buttons, with components for React. Appreciate Button does the like and clap part, free, open source and self-hostable. Which fits depends on what you need.

As of September 2026, from Lyket's pricing page.

Side by side

Lyket Appreciate Button
Price Free up to 500 pageviews a month; 4€ a month (billed yearly) up to 5,000; 10€ a month up to 50,000 Free, hosted or self-hosted
Limits Hard pageview limits below the custom plan None by plan; rate limits protect the server
Self-hosting Not offered on its pricing page Yes, Node.js and MySQL, Docker image included
Button types Like, clap and rating One button that counts several clicks, or one
Source Hosted service MIT licensed

When each one fits

  • Lyket, if you want rating buttons, or would rather pay for a service than think about hosting.
  • Appreciate Button, if your traffic would outgrow a free tier, you want the data on your own server, or you want your own icon that fills as readers click.

Trying it takes one tag: see the guides, or the like button page for a live demo.

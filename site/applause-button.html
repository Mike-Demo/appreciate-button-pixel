Applause button · open source · one tag

An applause button for every page of your site.

Give readers a way to applaud what they read. They click an icon that fills up with each clap, you see which pages people enjoyed, and there is no account, cookie or tracking pixel involved.

Add applause in one line

<script src="https://appreciate-button.com/widget.js" data-key="pk_YOUR_BUTTON_KEY" async></script>

The tag draws the button right where it sits. To place it yourself, or to put several on one page, load the script once and use the element: 

<script src="https://appreciate-button.com/widget.js" async></script>
<appreciator-button data-key="pk_YOUR_BUTTON_KEY"></appreciator-button>

What you can set for each button

  • How many claps each reader can give, from 1 to 1,000.
  • The icon: a heart, or your own SVG, recoloured for rest, hover, click and full.
  • What flies out on a click: copies of the icon, short dashes, or nothing.
  • Where the count sits, a thank-you message for readers who gave every clap, and sound.
  • Which sites may show it, so nobody else can use your key.

Every site's total can be shown as a badge, and sites are ranked on the most appreciated board unless their owner opts out.

Used the Applause Button before?

Its free hosted service is no longer running. Appreciator does the same job with a hosted instance and a dashboard, and swapping one tag for another is most of the move. How to move over.
